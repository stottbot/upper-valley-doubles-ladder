Rules — Season 1 (September 14, 2026 – January 22, 2027)

DRAFT for Jeff's edit. Wilmington Tennis Ladder core, trimmed for a 15–30-team doubles ladder. Free, volunteer-run.

Who and what

  • Men's 3.5 doubles. Teams of two (a third partner is allowed for scheduling flexibility). A team is one rung.
  • 18+. No fee, ever. Join any time by emailing the ladder address with both players' names, contact info, and NTRP ratings.
  • Play anywhere. Boss, Storrs Pond, Hartford High, CCBA, a town court, a backyard — whatever the four of you agree on. Default is a free court; if you choose a paid court, all four split it. The ladder has no home venue and needs nobody's permission.

Placement

  • New teams enter at the bottom. Your first challenge of the season may be to any rung so seeding mistakes fix themselves fast.
  • A partner change makes a new team: it enters three rungs below the higher-ranked member's old rung.

Challenges

  • Challenge any team within 5 rungs above or below you (7 once there are 21+ teams).
  • The challenging team makes contact and brings a new can of balls (winner keeps it).
  • Respond to a challenge within 48 hours. The challenged team offers at least three day/times, including one evening or weekend. Play within 14 days of accepting. A team that won't respond or won't offer times defaults the match.
  • Don't play the same opponents twice within 14 days.
  • Free challenge: once per season, challenge any team regardless of rung. Say "free challenge" when you report it. It cannot be won by forfeit.
  • After a loss, play a team below you and win before challenging up again (this keeps everyone moving; the commissioner will nudge, not punish, in season 1).

Results and movement

  • If the lower-ranked team wins, it takes the loser's rung and every team in between drops one. If the higher-ranked team wins, nothing moves.
  • Report every match within 24 hours: the winners email the ladder address, cc'ing the losers, with one line like Result: Federer/Nadal d. Borg/McEnroe 6-3 4-6 [10-7]. Standings update overnight.

Activity requirement (what keeps the ladder alive)

  • Every team plays at least 2 matches per period, against different opponents, at least one against a team below you. Periods: Sep 14 – Oct 31, November, Dec 1 – Jan 22.
  • Miss it and you drop 5 rungs at the start of the next period (7 once there are 21+ teams).
  • The bottom three teams are exempt from the "one down" part. Teams that join in the second half of a period are exempt for that period. Teams on leave for 14+ days of a period are exempt.

Format

  • Best of three sets, tiebreak at 6–6. A 10-point match tiebreak in place of a third set if both teams agree; decide before you start. An 8-game pro set is fine by agreement.
  • Forfeit: more than 15 minutes late, or unavailable at the agreed time (you may mutually reschedule instead). The forfeiting team gets no credit; the other team gets the win and the rung.
  • Rain, snow, or a temperature below 32 °F are the only reasons to cancel an outdoor match. An interrupted match resumes from where it stopped within 10 days.

Leave

  • Traveling or hurt? Email "freeze us" — your rung is held for up to 3 weeks and you can't be challenged (shown as <>). Longer than that and you come off the ladder, re-entering 5 rungs below your old spot when you're back.

Playoffs and prizes

  • The top four teams with at least 8 matches play off after January 22: 1 v 4, 2 v 3, then a final. Short sets (to 4) with a 10-point tiebreak at one set all.
  • Recognition for the winners and for the team that played the most matches ("the Tennis Bums").

Disputes

  • USTA rules govern play. The commissioner (Jeff Stott) settles anything else; the goal is more tennis, not more rules.
